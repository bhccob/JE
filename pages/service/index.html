Our 1-Week Fix Promise

When you bring in a product purchased from Jontai Energy with your original receipt, we commit to diagnosing and fixing it within 7 calendar days. Here's what that covers:

  • Diagnosis and assessment — at no charge when the product is under warranty
  • Repair or part replacement completed within 1 week of drop-off
  • If we cannot fix it in time, we will provide a direct replacement or full refund
  • You'll receive updates by phone or WhatsApp at every stage
  • Applies to solar panels, inverters, batteries, LED fixtures, and all hardware sold by us

* The 1-week promise applies to in-store repairs. On-site service appointments may have varying lead times depending on location and technician availability.

What We Service

Solar Panel Repair

Cracked cells, connection failures, output degradation, and junction box repairs by certified solar technicians.

Inverter & Battery Service

Fault diagnosis, firmware updates, component replacement, and full system testing for inverters and energy storage units.

LED Fixture Repair

Driver replacement, LED board swaps, heat sink cleaning, and waterproof seal restoration for commercial and residential fixtures.

Electrical Hardware

Circuit breaker, switchgear, and wiring diagnosis. Replacement of faulty components with like-for-like or upgraded parts.

Preventive Maintenance

Annual cleaning, connection torqueing, and performance checks for your solar or LED system to maximise lifespan and output.

On-Site Visits

Can't bring it in? Our technicians travel across Aruba for on-site diagnosis, repair, and panel cleaning.

How the Service Process Works
1

Bring in Your Product & Receipt

Visit us in-store during opening hours with the product and your original purchase receipt. No appointment needed for drop-offs.

2

Free Diagnosis

Our technician assesses the fault at no charge. If the repair is covered under warranty, all costs are zero to you. Out-of-warranty repairs are quoted upfront before any work begins — no surprises.

3

Repair Within 7 Days

Once you approve any quoted costs, we get to work. Most repairs are completed in 1–3 days. We'll contact you by phone or WhatsApp when it's ready — always within 7 days.

4

Pick Up & Test

Collect your product and we'll walk you through what was done. Every repaired unit is tested before hand-back. Repairs carry a 90-day workmanship guarantee.

5

If We Miss the 1-Week Promise

In the unlikely event we cannot complete the repair within 7 days, we will offer you a direct replacement product of equal value, or a full refund — your choice.
