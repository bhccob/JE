How the Service Process Works
1

Bring in Your Product & Receipt

Visit us in-store during opening hours with the product and your original purchase receipt. No appointment needed for drop-offs.

2

Free Diagnosis

Our technician assesses the fault at no charge when the issue is potentially covered by warranty. Out-of-warranty repairs are quoted upfront before any work begins.

3

Repair Plan Confirmed

Once diagnosis is complete, we confirm the repair plan, expected timeline, and any quoted cost. Most routine repairs are completed quickly, but the timeline depends on the exact fault and part availability.

4

Pick Up & Test

Collect your product and we'll walk you through what was done. Every repaired unit is tested before hand-back. Repairs carry a 90-day workmanship guarantee.

5

If Repair Is Delayed

If a repair takes longer than first expected, we update you on the reason, the revised timeline, and whether warranty, replacement, or return options should be considered instead.
