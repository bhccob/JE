The 1-Week Return & Exchange Policy

Any product purchased from Jontai Energy can be exchanged or returned for a full refund within 7 calendar days of the purchase date, provided you bring your original receipt. Here's what the policy covers:

  • Bring the product and receipt to our store — no prior appointment needed
  • Choose a direct exchange for the same or equivalent product, or a full refund to your original payment method
  • Products must be in original condition — unused and in original packaging for non-defective returns
  • Defective products can be returned or exchanged at any time within the warranty period, regardless of the 1-week window
  • Refunds are processed within 3 business days for cash and up to 7 business days for card payments

* Products that have been installed or modified on-site cannot be returned unless a manufacturing defect is confirmed by our technicians.

What Can Be Returned

Unopened / Unused Products

Any product in its original sealed packaging, returned within 7 days with receipt. Full refund or exchange — no questions asked.

Defective Products

Products that arrive faulty or develop a fault within the warranty period are eligible for free repair, exchange, or refund. Receipt required for the 1-week window; warranty card applies beyond that.

Wrong Item Received

If we made an error and you received the wrong product, we will replace it and cover any transport costs. Contact us immediately.

Opened but Unused Products

Opened products that are still in unused condition (no signs of installation or wear) may be exchanged within 7 days. A technician may inspect the item.

Installed Products (Non-defective)

Products that have been installed, wired, or mounted cannot be returned unless a manufacturing defect is confirmed.

Custom or Special-Order Items

Items ordered to specification (custom cable lengths, special voltages, etc.) are non-returnable unless they arrive defective.

Returns After 7 Days (Non-defective)

Non-defective products returned after 7 days of purchase are not eligible for refund or exchange. Defective products remain covered under the warranty period.

How to Return or Exchange
1

Bring the Product & Receipt In-Store

Visit us during opening hours with the product and your original purchase receipt. No appointment is needed. Our staff will assist you at the counter.

2

Quick Inspection

We'll briefly inspect the product to confirm its condition. For suspected defects, a technician may run a short diagnostic — this takes no more than 15–20 minutes in most cases.

3

Choose: Exchange or Refund

You choose what works best for you — a direct exchange for the same or equivalent product, or a full refund to your original payment method.

4

Refund Processed

Cash refunds are issued on the spot. Card refunds are processed within 3–7 business days depending on your bank. You'll receive a confirmation slip.

Need to return or exchange a product?

Check our opening hours or contact us if you have questions before visiting.
