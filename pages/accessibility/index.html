Accessibility

Accessibility at Jontai Energy

We are committed to making our store, website, and services accessible and welcoming to everyone — including customers with disabilities.

Our Commitment

Jontai Energy believes that every customer deserves equal access to our products, services, and information. We continuously work to ensure our physical store location and online presence meet the needs of all customers, including those with visual, auditory, motor, or cognitive disabilities.

If you encounter any barrier — on this website or in our store — please let us know. We take all accessibility feedback seriously and act on it.

In-Store Accessibility

Wheelchair & Mobility Access

Our store entrance and service counter are accessible to customers using wheelchairs or mobility aids. Staff are available to assist with reaching products on higher shelves.

Accessible Parking

Designated accessible parking spaces are available close to the store entrance. Please speak to a staff member if you need additional assistance from the parking area.

Staff Assistance

Our team is happy to provide personal shopping assistance, read product labels aloud, explain technical specifications, or help carry products to your vehicle.

Phone & WhatsApp Orders

Customers who find it difficult to visit in person can place orders or request product information by phone or WhatsApp. We offer delivery for qualifying orders within Aruba.

Website Accessibility

Text Resizing

All text on this website can be resized using your browser's built-in zoom controls (Ctrl + / Ctrl − on desktop, pinch-zoom on mobile) without loss of content or functionality.

Colour Contrast

We aim for sufficient colour contrast throughout the site so that text is readable for users with low vision or colour blindness. If you find any page difficult to read, please report it.

Keyboard Navigation

All interactive elements — navigation, forms, and buttons — are accessible via keyboard. Use Tab to move between elements and Enter or Space to activate them.

Language Options

This website is available in English, Spanish, and Chinese, with more languages planned. Use the language selector in the navigation bar to switch.

Mobile & Screen Reader Friendly

Pages are built with semantic HTML and ARIA labels to support screen readers. The site is fully responsive and tested on common mobile devices and browsers.

Accessibility Feedback

Report an Issue

If you experience any difficulty accessing content or using our services, contact us via our Contact page, by phone, or by WhatsApp. Describe the issue and the page or area affected — we will respond within 2 business days.

Continuous Improvement

We review and update our accessibility practices regularly. Feedback from customers is the most valuable input we receive — all suggestions are reviewed by our team.

Need help or want to give feedback?

Reach out to our team — we're happy to assist or hear your suggestions.
