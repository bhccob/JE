Who We Are

A Family Business Built on Clean Energy

Jontai Energy was built to make cleaner, more efficient energy infrastructure easier to access across Aruba. What started as a family-led mission grew into a business that helps homeowners and commercial clients plan smarter systems, reduce operating costs, and work with a team that stays accountable from consultation to handover.

Direct communication Clients work with a team that stays clear, responsive, and practical throughout the project.
Operational focus We think beyond products alone and look at energy use, reliability, and lifecycle value.
Long-term relationships Our goal is not a one-time sale but a reputation built on dependable follow-through.
Our Mission

To provide high-quality solar energy solutions that empower homes and businesses to reduce their carbon footprint while saving on energy costs.
