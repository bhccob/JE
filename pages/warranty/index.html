Warranty Coverage

Jontai Energy warrants that all products sold are free from defects in materials and workmanship under normal use and service conditions for the applicable warranty period. This warranty covers:

  • Manufacturing defects and material failures under normal operating conditions
  • Defects that arise despite correct installation and use in accordance with product specifications
  • Performance shortfalls where a product fails to meet its published specifications

Where a product carries a manufacturer warranty that is more favourable than our store warranty, the manufacturer's terms will apply and we will assist you in exercising those rights.

Exclusions

This warranty does not cover:

  • Damage caused by accidents, misuse, abuse, or negligence
  • Damage caused by installation not performed or supervised by a Jontai Energy technician or approved installer
  • Damage from unauthorised modifications, repairs, or tampering
  • Normal wear and tear, including surface scratches, fading, or cosmetic deterioration
  • Damage from lightning strikes, power surges, flooding, salt air corrosion beyond specified tolerances, or other acts of nature
  • Products with removed, altered, or illegible serial numbers
  • Consumable parts (e.g., fuses, bulbs in non-LED fixtures) that wear out under normal use
  • Software, firmware, or configuration issues not related to a hardware defect

Making a Warranty Claim

To make a warranty claim:

  1. Contact us via our contact page, by phone, or visit us in-store.
  2. Provide proof of purchase â€” your original receipt or invoice, and the product serial number where applicable.
  3. Describe the defect â€” include photos or video where possible to help us assess the issue quickly.
  4. We will assess the claim within 5 business days and contact you with the outcome and next steps.
  5. Do not attempt repair yourself or through a third party before contacting us, as this may void the warranty.

For installed systems (solar, LED, etc.), our technician may arrange an on-site inspection at no cost if the defect is covered under warranty.

5

Remedies

Where a valid warranty claim is accepted, we will at our discretion:

  • Repair the defective product at no cost to you
  • Replace the product with the same or equivalent model
  • Refund the purchase price if repair or replacement is not feasible

Replacement products carry the balance of the original warranty period or 90 days from the replacement date, whichever is longer. We cover reasonable labour and transport costs within Aruba for valid in-warranty repairs.

6

Installation Warranty

When you purchase installation services from Jontai Energy, our technicians' workmanship is warranted for 1 year from the installation date. This covers:

  • Faulty wiring connections made by our team
  • Improper mounting that causes product failure
  • System configuration errors made during installation

This installation warranty is separate from and in addition to the product warranty. If a defect results from both a product fault and an installation issue, we will coordinate the resolution at no cost to you.

7

Statutory Rights

This warranty is offered in addition to your statutory rights under Aruban law. As a consumer in Aruba, you have the right to receive goods that conform to the contract of sale. If a product does not conform at the time of delivery, you are entitled to repair, replacement, price reduction, or rescission of the contract under the Burgerlijk Wetboek van Aruba.

Nothing in this warranty policy limits those statutory rights. If our warranty period has expired but a product fails within a timeframe that suggests an inherent defect at the time of sale, your statutory rights may still apply.
