Privacy Policy

How Jontai Energy collects, uses, and protects your personal information in accordance with Aruban law.

Last updated: March 19, 2026  ·  Effective: March 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Jontai Energy N.V., a company incorporated in Aruba under the laws of Aruba, and governs the processing of personal data in accordance with the Landsverordening bescherming persoonsgegevens (LBP) - Aruba's Personal Data Protection Ordinance.

1

Who We Are

Jontai Energy N.V. is a hardware and energy solutions retailer based in Aruba, specialising in solar panels, LED lighting systems, and related electrical hardware. We operate retail services in-store and online.

Data Controller: Jontai Energy N.V., Aruba
Contact: Contact Us

2

Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data when you interact with us in-store, online, or by phone:

  • Identification data: full name, ID or passport number (for warranty registrations and large purchases as required by Aruban law)
  • Contact data: email address, phone number, home or business address
  • Transaction data: purchase history, invoices, product serial numbers, payment method type (we do not store full card numbers)
  • Warranty & service data: installation address, product details, service appointment information
  • Quote request data: property type, energy usage, roof specifications
  • Career data: CV, cover letter, references (job applicants only)
  • Website data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, cookie identifiers
  • Communications: emails, WhatsApp messages, or forms you send us

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe we have done so in error, please contact us immediately.

3

How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling purchases, orders, and deliveries
  • Providing installation and after-sales service for solar and LED products
  • Managing warranties and product registrations
  • Responding to customer service enquiries and support requests
  • Sending product updates, promotions, and newsletters (with your consent)
  • Processing job applications
  • Complying with Aruban legal obligations including tax and import regulations
  • Improving our website and services through anonymised analytics
4

Legal Basis for Processing

Under the Landsverordening bescherming persoonsgegevens (LBP), we process your data on the following legal grounds:

  • Contract performance: processing necessary to complete a sale, delivery, installation, or service
  • Legal obligation: compliance with tax law, import duties, or other Aruban regulations
  • Legitimate interests: fraud prevention, business operations, and website security
  • Consent: marketing communications and non-essential cookies - you may withdraw consent at any time
5

Sharing of Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only with:

  • Delivery and logistics partners in Aruba for order fulfilment
  • Installation subcontractors in Aruba, solely to complete your service
  • Payment processors for secure transaction handling (they receive only what is necessary)
  • Aruban government authorities (DIMP, Customs, etc.) when legally required
  • IT service providers who host or maintain our systems, under strict data processing agreements

Any third party with whom we share data is bound by confidentiality obligations and may not use your data for any purpose beyond what we authorise.

6

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary:

  • Transaction & invoice records: 7 years (Aruban tax and accounting requirements)
  • Warranty records: duration of warranty period plus 2 years
  • Marketing preferences: until you withdraw consent or 3 years of inactivity
  • Job applications (unsuccessful): 6 months, then securely deleted
  • Website analytics: 12 months in anonymised form

After the applicable retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

7

Your Rights

Under Aruban data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access - request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction - request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion - request erasure of your data, where no legal obligation requires us to retain it
  • Objection - object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Restriction - request that we restrict processing in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent - at any time for consent-based processing such as marketing emails

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via our Contact page. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Bureau Toezicht Telecommunicatie (BTT), Aruba's supervisory authority for data protection matters.

8

Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. These include:

  • HTTPS encryption on all web communications
  • Access controls limiting data to authorised staff only
  • Secure storage of physical documents at our Aruba premises
  • Regular review of data handling practices

In the event of a data breach that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

9

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve your experience. We use:

  • Essential cookies: required for the site to function correctly (e.g., language preference)
  • Analytics cookies: anonymised data to understand how visitors use the site - only with your consent

When you first visit the site, a cookie notice lets you accept all cookies, keep essential-only storage, or manage preferences. Essential storage remains active because it supports core site functions. Any future analytics tooling will remain off until you opt in.

You can manage or disable non-essential cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in Aruban law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Continued use of our services after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
