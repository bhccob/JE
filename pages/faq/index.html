What types of solar panels do you offer?

We offer a comprehensive range of high-quality solar panels including monocrystalline, polycrystalline, and thin-film panels. Each type has its own advantages, and our experts can help you choose the best option based on your energy needs, budget, and available space.

What is the efficiency rating of your solar panels?

Our solar panels range from 18% to 22% efficiency, depending on the model. Our premium monocrystalline panels offer the highest efficiency ratings, making them ideal for maximizing energy production in limited space.

Do you provide LED lighting solutions?

Yes! In addition to solar panels, we offer energy-efficient LED lighting solutions for both residential and commercial applications. Our LED products can significantly reduce your energy consumption and complement your solar system perfectly.

How long does installation take?

A typical residential solar panel installation takes 1-3 days, depending on the system size and complexity. This includes mounting the panels, installing the inverter, and connecting to your electrical system. Commercial installations may take longer based on scale.

Do you provide installation services or just products?

We offer complete installation services through our network of certified installers. We can also provide products only if you prefer to work with your own installer. Our team ensures proper installation for optimal performance and warranty coverage.

Will solar panels work on my roof type?

Solar panels can be installed on most roof types including asphalt shingles, metal, tile, and flat roofs. Our installation experts will assess your roof's condition, orientation, and structural integrity to determine the best mounting solution for your specific situation.

How much does a solar panel system cost?

The cost varies based on system size, panel type, and installation complexity. A typical residential system ranges from $15,000 to $30,000 before incentives. However, federal tax credits and local rebates can significantly reduce this cost. Contact us for a personalized quote.

Are there financing options available?

Yes, we offer multiple financing options including solar loans, leases, and power purchase agreements (PPAs). These options allow you to start saving on energy costs with little to no upfront investment. Our team can help you find the best financing solution for your budget.

What incentives and rebates are available?

The federal solar Investment Tax Credit (ITC) currently offers a 30% tax credit on solar installations. Many states and utilities also offer additional rebates and incentives. Our team stays updated on all available programs and will help you maximize your savings.

How much maintenance do solar panels require?

Solar panels require minimal maintenance. Generally, rainfall keeps them clean, but periodic cleaning (2-4 times per year) can optimize performance. We recommend annual inspections to check connections and ensure everything is working properly.

What is the lifespan of solar panels?

Our solar panels are built to last 25-30 years or more. Most manufacturers offer 25-year performance warranties, guaranteeing at least 80% of original output after 25 years. The panels continue to produce energy beyond this period, albeit at slightly reduced efficiency.

What happens if a panel gets damaged?

Our solar panels come with comprehensive warranties covering manufacturing defects and performance. Many homeowners insurance policies also cover damage from weather events. If a panel is damaged, we can quickly replace it, and the rest of your system will continue functioning normally.

How much can I save on my electricity bill?

Savings depend on your energy consumption, system size, and local electricity rates. On average, homeowners save 50-90% on their electricity bills. Many customers eliminate their electricity bills entirely. We provide detailed savings projections during the consultation process.

Do solar panels work on cloudy days?

Yes, solar panels still generate electricity on cloudy days, though at reduced capacity (typically 10-25% of optimal output). Modern panels are designed to capture both direct and diffused sunlight, ensuring consistent energy production even in less-than-ideal weather conditions.

Can I store excess energy produced?

Absolutely! We offer battery storage solutions that allow you to store excess energy generated during the day for use at night or during power outages. Popular options include Tesla Powerwall and other high-capacity lithium-ion battery systems. Alternatively, many areas offer net metering programs.
