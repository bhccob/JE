About Jontai Energy

Leading the solar revolution in Aruba

Our Story

Jontai Energy is a family-owned business founded with a simple mission: to make sustainable solar energy accessible to everyone in Aruba. We believe in a future powered by clean, renewable energy that benefits both our customers and the environment. As a family business, we bring personal dedication and care to every project we undertake.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality solar energy solutions that empower homes and businesses to reduce their carbon footprint while saving on energy costs.

Our Vision

A sustainable future where every building in Aruba is powered by clean, renewable solar energy.
