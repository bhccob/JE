## Scope

Home page (`app/page.tsx`), Persuade mode. Audience: Aruban households and small businesses choosing solar, backup power, lighting, cameras or furniture. Action: explore a category, ask on WhatsApp, get directions to Cumana 73. No checkout.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One day of Aruban sun. The scroll is the clock: the sun crosses one lime-plaster wall from dawn to the morning after, and each product does its job at its hour (the panel at first light, the inverter at noon, the solar AC in the heat, the power station in late sun, the LED bulb at dusk, the camera at night, the generator in an outage), then morning light rises to the furniture floor. It refuses the solar-store default of a stock panel photo over a grid of product cards, and the v2 default of real-time toy models on flat colour fields.

OWN-WORLD: One rendered set, never the real store: warm lime plaster, terrazzo, light oak (ledge, plinths, mezzanine), the wind-bent divi-divi shadow. Sand paper and forest ink around the film; day colours only (the owner dropped the day/night switch on 2026-10-27); frosted glass as a finish, not boxes. Gambetta display, Hanken Grotesk body. Pill controls, 28px panels, circles for marks. Sun amber exists only as light (the dial, the ring, the stamp).

STORY: The page opens on a sunrise that plays once, then scroll moves the sun. Each chapter's copy holds while its product arrives, then slides out as the camera leaves for the next. At dusk the visitor relights the bulb from 3000K to 6500K. The night passes on the generator, the camera climbs past the oak soffit into morning upstairs, then loyalty and the visit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Two-line Gambetta headline "Aruba sunlight, made useful." left, one line, "Explore solar" filled and "Visit the store" outlined, over the golden dawn frame: the divi-divi shadow on plaster and the solar panel on its oak post right of centre.

FORM: Owner-chosen concept A of three, Blender plus Higgsfield production, style frames approved by the owner on 2026-10-26 before the full render. Signature: the scroll-scrubbed day itself; secondary interactions are the bulb slider and the day/night switch.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
